        How it works

        • Post a job in the channel: where it is, what it needs, a photo. It gets a card with a number (AMS, RCH or SND).
        • ✅ the card when it's done. Reply on the card with anything else.
        • Say what you used ("used 6 bags of mulch"), what arrived ("10 bags dropped off") or what's left ("3 boxes left"). Stock here follows.
        • Out of something? Say so in the channel and it goes on order.
