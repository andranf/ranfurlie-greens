    Where the numbers come from

      How the service plan works

      Hours. Telematics machines (Reelmaster 5010-Hs, eTriFlex 2, Workman GTX 2) report their hour meter to Toro Intelli360 and are read every hour. Every other machine is projected from its last reading at the rate it has been used on the job board; ~ marks an estimate. A meter reading in a worklist reply resets it.

      What's due. Each machine's OEM schedule (operator's manual, in the machinery bible) is counted from the last time that service, or a bigger one, was recorded. Break-in services count from new. Club practice (backlap, grind, greasing, blades) carries over the old taskTracker intervals.

      No record. A machine bought before 2024 with no record of a service is assumed to have had it at the last multiple of the interval; a newer one is counted from new, so a missing record shows as overdue.

      Closing a job. Tick the worklist card in #machinery and reply with the hours, or press Update on the job on the shared link: Done, In progress, Waiting on parts, Needs approval or a note, with the time spent and the hour meter reading. Updates post to #machinery straight away; the hourly run logs finished work and the item drops off.
