Kobelco 3.6t Excavator

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: Safe Operation of Kobelco Excavator
Make / Model: Kobelco 3.6t Excavator
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. Potential Hazards

  • Machine Rollover: Risk of tipping or rolling over, especially on uneven terrain or slopes.
  • Crush Injuries: Danger of being caught between the excavator and other objects.
  • Striking Overhead or Underground Utilities: Contact with power lines, pipes, or other buried utilities.
  • Flying Debris: Rocks or materials can be ejected during digging or movement.
  • Pinch Points and Moving Parts: Risk of entanglement in the tracks, boom, or bucket.
  • Pressure-Related Hazards: High-pressure hydraulic systems pose risks of leaks or fluid injection injuries.
  • Manual Handling: Risk of injury from handling heavy or awkward components.
  • Environmental Contamination: Potential for leaks or spills of fuel, oils, or hydraulic fluids.

2. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Steel-capped boots with slip-resistant soles.
  • High-visibility clothing.
  • Safety glasses.
  • Hard hat when working near overhead hazards.
  • Gloves for handling equipment and adjusting.
  • Hearing protection if noise levels exceed safe limits.

3. Pre-Operational Checks

1. Excavator Inspection:

  • Conduct a thorough pre-start inspection as outlined in the asset’s manual.
  • Check fuel, hydraulic fluid, and engine oil levels.
  • Inspect hoses, tracks, and buckets for damage, wear, or leaks.
  • Ensure all safety devices, such as seat belts, warning lights, and alarms, are functional.
  • Verify the Roll-Overprotective Structure (ROPS) is in good condition.

2. Area Inspection:

  • Consult Dial Before You Dig (DBYD) plans to identify underground utilities and mark their locations.
  • Assess ground stability, avoiding operations in soft or uneven terrain.
  • Check for overhead hazards like power lines, ensuring compliance with minimum clearance distances.

3. Operator Readiness:

  • Ensure operators are trained, competent, and fit for work.
  • Operators must be free from the influence of alcohol, drugs, or medications that impair performance.

4. Documentation:

  • Record all pre-start checks in Task Tracker.
  • Review and acknowledge the Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS) or this SWP before starting.

4. Operation

1. Starting the Excavator:

  • Park on level ground with the parking brake engaged.
  • Inspect the work area for unauthorized personnel.
  • Fasten the seatbelt and adjust the seat for safe operation.
  • Allow the engine to warm up before engaging controls.

2. Safe Operation Practices:

  • Operate the excavator within the manufacturer’s guidelines and rated capacities.
  • Maintain three points of contact when entering or exiting the cab.
  • Keep bystanders and other workers clear of the swing radius.
  • Always use the bucket or boom to stabilize the machine when operating on slopes.

3. Trenching and Digging:

  • Dig gradually, placing spoil piles away from trench edges to reduce the risk of collapse.
  • Use spotters when visibility is restricted or when operating near underground services.

4. Hydraulic System Safety:

  • Keep body parts away from hydraulic lines during operation.
  • Inspect hoses regularly for leaks or wear and repair them before operation.

5. Environmental Considerations:

  • Prevent contamination by avoiding spills of fuel, oils, or hydraulic fluids.
  • Use absorbent materials from spill kits immediately if leaks occur.

5. Post-Operational Procedures

1. Shutdown:

  • Park the excavator on level ground and lower the bucket or attachments to the ground.
  • Disengage controls and turn off the engine.
  • Remove the key and engage the parking brake before exiting.

2. Cleaning and Maintenance:

  • Clean tracks and attachments to remove debris.
  • Inspect for any new damage or leaks and report these immediately.
  • Log any maintenance or repairs in Task Tracker.

3. Storage:

  • Store in a secure location away from unauthorized access.
  • Ensure all hatches and compartments are closed and locked.

6. Maintenance and Servicing

1. Scheduled Maintenance:

  • All maintenance and servicing of the Kobelco 3.6t excavator will be conducted through Melbourne Tractors.
  • Maintenance schedules must align with the manufacturer’s recommendations and logged in Task Tracker.

2. Pre-Service Preparations:

  • Ensure the excavator is clean and free from excessive dirt or debris before servicing.
  • Provide detailed reports of any identified issues to Melbourne Tractors before scheduled servicing.

3. Post-Service Records:

  • Verify and record all servicing completed by Melbourne Tractors in Task Tracker.
  • Maintain copies of service reports for compliance and operational review.

7. Emergency Procedures

1. Machine Rollover:

  • Remain inside the cab with the seatbelt fastened until the machine stops moving.
  • Shut off the engine and call for assistance.

2. Contact with Utilities:

  • Immediately stop operations and notify the relevant utility company.
  • Evacuate if there is a risk of fire or electric shock.

3. Injury Response:

  • Provide first aid and call emergency services for severe injuries.
  • Document the incident and notify the site supervisor.

8. Relevant Risk Controls (from Asset Risk Assessment)

  • Pre-Start Inspections: Conducted thoroughly to identify any missing safety devices or potential hazards.
  • Hydraulic Safety: Inspect hoses for wear or leaks to prevent high-pressure fluid injuries.
  • Environmental Protection: Spill kits must be available and used for any leaks.
  • Overhead and Underground Hazards: Consult DBYD for underground services and maintain safe clearance distances for overhead utilities.
  • Competency: Only qualified operators or those under direct supervision may operate the excavator.

9. Record Keeping

1. Task Tracker:

  • Record all pre-start checks, faults, and servicing activities.
  • Maintain accurate logs of operational hours and tasks completed.

2. Service Reports:

  • Document all services conducted by Melbourne Tractors and retain records for compliance.
