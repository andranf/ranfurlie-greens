Handheld Sprayers

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: Handheld Chemical Spraying
Make / Model: Birchmeier / Milwaukee Battery Sprayers
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. Potential Hazards

  • Chemical exposure: Direct contact with herbicides, fungicides, and insecticides through skin absorption, inhalation, or ingestion. Many products cause skin irritation, respiratory damage, or systemic poisoning.
  • Eye injury: Spray drift or splash can cause chemical burns to the eyes.
  • Allergic reaction: Some operators may develop sensitisation to turf chemicals over time.
  • Ergonomic strain: Carrying a full backpack sprayer (10-15 kg) for extended periods strains back and shoulders.
  • Slip and fall: Operating on wet turf, slopes, and around bunkers.
  • Battery hazard (Milwaukee models): Lithium battery risks per the Lithium-Ion SWP.
  • Environmental contamination: Overspray or spills near waterways, drains, or native areas.

2. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Chemical-resistant gloves (mandatory).
  • Safety glasses or face shield (mandatory).
  • Long-sleeve shirt and long trousers (mandatory).
  • Chemical-resistant apron or coveralls for concentrated mixing.
  • Appropriate respirator (P2 minimum; check the product SDS for specific requirements).
  • Steel-capped boots (mandatory).
  • Sun hat and SPF 50+ sunscreen.

3. Pre-Operational Checks

a. Machine Inspection

  • Complete pre-start via Task Tracker QR.
  • Read the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for every product being applied.
  • Inspect the sprayer: check for leaks, cracked hoses, and nozzle condition.
  • Verify the nozzle type and pressure produce the correct application rate.
  • Calibrate the sprayer if not recently done.
  • Check the wind speed and direction; do not spray in winds above 15 km/h.
  • For battery models: check battery charge and condition.

b. Work Area

  • Mark the application area if needed.
  • Confirm no golfers or public are in the spray zone.
  • Identify buffer zones near waterways, drains, and sensitive areas.
  • Ensure the chemical mixing area SWP has been followed for mixing.

4. Operation

a. Start-Up

  • Fill the tank at the designated mixing area only.
  • Pressurise (manual pump) or switch on (battery).
  • Test spray on a non-sensitive area to confirm coverage and rate.

b. During Operation

  • Apply the product at the rate specified on the label and the superintendent's spray sheet.
  • Maintain a consistent walking speed for even coverage.
  • Watch for spray drift; stop if wind picks up or shifts toward people, waterways, or sensitive areas.
  • Do not eat, drink, or smoke while spraying.
  • Wash hands and face before eating or drinking after spraying.
  • If the sprayer leaks during use, stop immediately, contain the spill, and follow the Spill Procedure SWP.

c. Golf Course Specific Rules

  • Spray per the superintendent's program.
  • Respect re-entry intervals (REI) on the product label.
  • Post signage on treated areas as required by the label.
  • Notify the pro shop and other staff of treated areas.

5. Emergency Procedures

a. Fire

  • Stop spraying. Decontaminate if safe. Extinguisher. Call 000.

b. Injury

  • Stop spraying. Remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with water.
  • If chemical in eyes: flush with water for 15 minutes.
  • Call the Poisons Information Centre: 13 11 26.
  • Call 000 if breathing difficulty or severe reaction.
  • Site: 825 Cranbourne-Frankston Rd, Cranbourne VIC 3977.
  • Report in Task Tracker.

6. Post-Operational Procedures

  • Triple-rinse the sprayer tank with clean water. Spray rinsate over the treated area.
  • Clean nozzles and filters.
  • Store the sprayer in the chemical shed. Report faults in Task Tracker.

7. Maintenance and Servicing

  • After each use: clean thoroughly, inspect nozzles and seals.
  • Replace nozzles when worn (affects application rate).
  • Battery care per Lithium-Ion SWP. Record in Task Tracker.

8. Relevant Legislation

  • Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic).
  • Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic).
  • AS/NZS 4024.1 Safety of Machinery series.
  • EPA Victoria noise regulations - Environment Protection Act 2017.
  • Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations 2012 (Vic) where applicable.

9. Record Keeping

  • Pre-start checklists completed daily via Task Tracker QR system.
  • All faults, repairs, and servicing recorded in Task Tracker.
  • Incident and near-miss reports submitted through Task Tracker.
  • Training records maintained for all operators with superintendent sign-off.
