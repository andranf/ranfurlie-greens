RMS Autonomous Mower Unit

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: RMS Autonomous Mowing Unit Procedures
Make / Model: RMS Unit
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. General Safety Guidelines

  • Only trained and authorized personnel are permitted to operate the mower.
  • Do not operate the machine while fatigued or under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Keep bystanders, children, and animals out of the operating area.
  • Always conduct a pre-operation risk assessment.
  • Shut off the engine, engage the parking brake, and remove the key before leaving the operator’s seat.
  • Adhere to all manufacturer instructions, safety decals, and this SWP.

2. Pre-Operation Checklist

Manual and Autonomous Use

1. Site Inspection:

  • Remove all debris, including rocks, branches, and loose objects.
  • Identify and mark potential hazards (e.g., slopes, bunkers, water hazards).

2. Machine Inspection:

  • Check blades, bolts, and cutting assemblies for wear or damage; replace as necessary.
  • Inspect hydraulic systems for leaks and ensure secure connections.
  • Verify that all guards and safety decals are intact and visible.

3. Fuel and Fluids:

  • Refuel the machine with the engine off in a well-ventilated area.
  • Check engine oil, coolant, and hydraulic fluid levels.

4. Safety Features:

  • Confirm the operation of lights, horn, reverse buzzer, and parking brake.
  • Test emergency stop functionality (manual or autonomous).

3. Operation

Manual Operation

1. Ensure the mower is on level ground with the parking brake engaged before starting.

2. Operate only from the designated operator seat; never allow passengers.

3. Mow only in daylight or well-lit conditions.

4. Avoid sharp turns, sudden stops, and operating on slopes beyond the mower’s capacity.

5. Use reduced speeds when manoeuvring near hazards or on uneven ground.

4. Autonomous Use

Overview

The RMS provides precision and efficiency by enabling autonomous mowing using GNSS technology. It ensures consistency, obstacle detection, and defined boundary adherence.

Legal Compliance

  • RMS operation must comply with:
  • Victorian Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004.
  • AS/NZS 4024.1:2019 Safety of Machinery.
  • Victorian Road Safety Act 1986 (prohibits public road operation).
  • Operators must maintain detailed training, risk assessment, and maintenance records.

Autonomous Pre-Operation Checklist

1. System Initialization:

  • Power on the RMS terminal and allow it to boot fully (approx. 60 seconds).
  • Verify GNSS signal strength with a minimum of one green bar for RTK accuracy.

2. Route Setup:

  • Load or review predefined mowing and transport routes using the RMS Planner.
  • Confirm boundaries, nodes, and transport routes are free of obstacles.

3. Inspection:

  • Inspect sensors, GNSS antennas, and cameras for dirt, damage, or obstructions.
  • Confirm the obstacle detection system is operational.

4. Supervisor Preparation:

  • Carry the RMS emergency remote for manual intervention.
  • Wear high-visibility clothing and remain within supervision range of the mower.

Autonomous Operation

1. Startup:

  • Position the mower within a defined node or start area.
  • Engage the parking brake and confirm safety preconditions via the RMS terminal.

2. Mowing:

  • Activate the route and monitor operation remotely using the TurfConnect App or visually supervise.
  • RMS will pause for detected obstacles; clear them to resume mowing.

3. Emergency Intervention:

  • Use the red button on the remote to halt the mower immediately if necessary.
  • Use the black button to shut down the entire system in emergencies.

4. Slope and Hazard Management:

  • Travel up and down slopes; avoid cross-slope operation unless programmed.
  • Use reduced speeds for tight turns or near hazard zones.

5. Post-Operation Checklist

Manual and Autonomous Use

1. Shutdown:

  • Lower cutting units, disengage power, and stop the engine.
  • Power down the RMS terminal and remove the key.

2. Inspection:

  • Check for damage to blades, sensors, or GNSS components.
  • Clean debris from cutting units and mower surfaces.

3. Fault Reporting:

  • Log any issues in the maintenance log and report them to management.

4. Storage:

  • Park the mower in a secure, designated area.
  • Ensure it is clean and ready for future use.

Maintenance Guidelines

1. Routine Maintenance:

  • Follow manufacturer recommendations for oil changes, hydraulic system checks, and blade replacements.
  • Regularly calibrate GNSS systems and obstacle detection sensors.

2. Hydraulic and RMS Components:

  • Inspect hoses for leaks and wear; replace as needed.
  • Clean GNSS antennas, cameras, and the RMS terminal regularly.

3. Software Updates:

  • Ensure the RMS software is up to date to maintain compatibility and safety standards.
