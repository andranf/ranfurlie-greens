Husqvarna LC347IVX Battery Mower

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: Battery-Powered Walk-Behind Mowing
Make / Model: Husqvarna LC347IVX
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. Potential Hazards

  • Rotating blade: The blade spins at high speed under the deck. No engine noise warns bystanders it is active; battery mowers are near-silent, increasing the risk of someone approaching unaware.
  • Ejected debris: Objects thrown from under the deck at speed can injure operators, bystanders, or golfers.
  • Lithium-ion battery hazard: Damaged, punctured, or overheated batteries can enter thermal runaway, releasing toxic gases and flames that cannot be extinguished with water alone.
  • Electrical shock: Damaged battery terminals or wiring can deliver a shock. Water ingress into the battery compartment increases this risk.
  • Silent operation risk: Pedestrians and golfers may not hear the mower approaching. Extra vigilance is required when mowing near paths and tee areas.
  • Manual handling: Battery packs are heavy (3-5 kg each) and awkward; dropping a battery can damage it and create a thermal event risk.
  • Vibration: Hand-arm vibration from extended operation.
  • Slope instability: Same slope risks as petrol walk-behind mowers.

2. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Steel-capped or composite-toe boots (mandatory).
  • Safety glasses (mandatory).
  • Hearing protection (recommended, though quieter than petrol models).
  • Sun hat and SPF 50+ sunscreen.
  • Long trousers (mandatory).
  • Nitrile gloves when handling batteries.

3. Pre-Operational Checks

a. Machine Inspection

  • Complete pre-start checklist via Task Tracker QR system.
  • Inspect the battery: check for cracks, swelling, leaks, or heat. Do not use a damaged battery.
  • Ensure the battery is fully charged and securely inserted.
  • Inspect the blade for sharpness and secure mounting.
  • Test the blade brake: the blade must stop within 3 seconds of releasing the bail lever.
  • Check the height-of-cut setting.

b. Work Area

  • Walk the area to remove debris and hazards.
  • Be especially aware of bystander proximity due to the quiet operation.

4. Operation

a. Start-Up

  • Insert battery, hold the safety bail, and press the start button.
  • Visually scan the area before engaging the blade.

b. During Operation

  • Be vigilant for pedestrians who may not hear the mower.
  • Do not mow in heavy rain; water ingress can damage the motor and battery.
  • If the mower stops unexpectedly, check the battery connection before troubleshooting.
  • Do not force the mower through thick, wet grass; reduce speed or raise the HOC.

c. Golf Course Specific Rules

  • Yield to golfers. Direct discharge away from paths and people.
  • The quiet operation is an advantage near clubhouse areas during events.

5. Emergency Procedures

Battery Thermal Event

  • If the battery starts smoking, hissing, or emitting an unusual odour, immediately remove it from the mower if safe. Place it on a non-flammable surface (concrete, gravel) away from buildings.
  • Do not use water to extinguish a lithium-ion battery fire. Use a dry chemical or CO2 extinguisher, or sand.
  • Evacuate the area and call 000. Notify the superintendent.
  • Refer to the Lithium-Ion Batteries and Equipment SWP for detailed battery emergency procedures.

Injury

  • Stop machine. Administer first aid. Call 000 for serious injuries.

  • Report in Task Tracker.

6. Post-Operational Procedures

  • Release the bail lever, wait for the blade to stop, then remove the battery.
  • Clean the mower at the wash-down bay. Do not direct water at the battery compartment.
  • Return the battery to the designated charging station in the maintenance facility.
  • Park the mower in its designated bay. Report faults in Task Tracker.

7. Maintenance and Servicing

  • Daily: clean machine, inspect blade.
  • Weekly: sharpen blade, inspect battery contacts for corrosion.
  • Store batteries in a cool, dry location away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
  • Follow the Lithium-Ion Batteries and Equipment SWP for all battery charging and storage.
  • All servicing recorded in Task Tracker.

8. Relevant Legislation

  • Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic).
  • Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic).
  • AS/NZS 4024.1 Safety of Machinery.
  • AS/NZS 62619 Lithium battery safety requirements.

9. Record Keeping

  • Pre-start checklists via Task Tracker QR system.
  • Faults, servicing, and battery replacements recorded in Task Tracker.
  • Incident and near-miss reports submitted through Task Tracker.
