Toro ProCore 648s

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: Walk-Behind Aerator Operation
Make / Model: Toro ProCore 648s
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. Potential Hazards

  • Tine ejection: Hollow or solid tines can break and eject under stress.
  • Crush injuries: The reciprocating mechanism is powerful enough to fracture bones.
  • Noise: Exceeds 85 dB(A).
  • Vibration: Intense hand-arm vibration during operation.
  • Ejected cores/debris: Soil cores and stones can be thrown.
  • Manual handling: Machine is heavy and awkward to manoeuvre, especially loading/unloading from trailers.
  • Ground damage: Can damage shallow irrigation lines if depth is not properly set.

2. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Steel-capped boots (mandatory).
  • Hearing protection (mandatory).
  • Safety glasses (mandatory).
  • Gloves when handling tines.
  • Sun hat and SPF 50+ sunscreen.

3. Pre-Operational Checks

a. Machine Inspection

  • Complete pre-start via Task Tracker QR.
  • Inspect tines for wear, cracks, and secure mounting.
  • Check hydraulic oil level.
  • Verify tine depth and spacing settings.
  • Test operator presence controls.

b. Work Area

  • Walk the area for debris and irrigation heads.
  • Check irrigation plans for shallow infrastructure.
  • Confirm area is clear of golfers.

4. Operation

a. Start-Up

  • Position on the aeration area. Start engine.
  • Engage tines and begin first pass slowly.

b. During Operation

  • Operate at a steady pace.
  • Lift tines before turning.
  • Keep personnel clear.
  • Never reach under the machine while running.
  • Monitor for unusual vibration or tine failure.

c. Golf Course Specific Rules

  • Aerate per superintendent schedule.
  • Coordinate with pro shop. Clean up cores as directed.

5. Emergency Procedures

a. Fire

  • Stop engine. Use extinguisher. Call 000.

b. Injury

  • Stop engine. First aid.
  • Call 000. Site: 825 Cranbourne-Frankston Rd, Cranbourne VIC 3977.
  • Report in Task Tracker.

6. Post-Operational Procedures

  • Turn off engine. Clean machine.
  • Inspect tines after use.
  • Park in designated bay. Report faults in Task Tracker.

7. Maintenance and Servicing

  • Daily: clean, inspect tines.
  • Replace worn tines. Record in Task Tracker.

8. Relevant Legislation

  • Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic).
  • Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic).
  • AS/NZS 4024.1 Safety of Machinery series.
  • EPA Victoria noise regulations - Environment Protection Act 2017.
  • Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations 2012 (Vic) where applicable.

9. Record Keeping

  • Pre-start checklists completed daily via Task Tracker QR system.
  • All faults, repairs, and servicing recorded in Task Tracker.
  • Incident and near-miss reports submitted through Task Tracker.
  • Training records maintained for all operators with superintendent sign-off.
Disclaimer: This SWP may not cover all possible hazards associated with this equipment on your specific site and should be used in conjunction with other references including the Operational or User Manual. It is designed as a guide, a safety reminder, and a training support document. Users must undertake an activity risk assessment prior to using any equipment and receive training in its safe use before operation.
