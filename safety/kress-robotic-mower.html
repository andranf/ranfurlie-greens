Kress KR233 Robotic Mower

SWP Issue: Nov 2026 Review: Nov 2027
Description: Autonomous Robotic Mowing
Make / Model: Kress KR233 RTKn
⚠ DO NOT operate this equipment unless you have been fully trained in its safe use and operation, have read the user manual, and have conducted a risk assessment using the Task Tracker QR system.

1. Potential Hazards

  • Blade contact: The robotic mower has rotating blades under its body. While equipped with lift and tilt sensors, there is a residual risk of blade contact during maintenance, manual handling, or if safety sensors fail.
  • Crush/entrapment: The mower is heavy (approx. 25 kg) and can trap fingers or toes if it runs over a foot or pins a hand during lifting.
  • Lithium-ion battery: Contains a large lithium battery pack. Risk of thermal runaway if the battery is damaged, punctured, or exposed to extreme heat.
  • Electrical hazard: Charging contacts and internal wiring carry current. Water ingress or damaged insulation can cause shock.
  • Silent operation: The mower operates near-silently. Golfers, maintenance staff, and visitors may not notice it approaching.
  • Collision with infrastructure: If boundary wires or RTK signal are disrupted, the mower may contact irrigation heads, bunker edges, garden beds, or course furniture.
  • Interference with play: The mower may operate during active play. Golfers may be distracted or trip over it if unaware of its presence.
  • Theft/vandalism: Robotic mowers left unattended on the course may be stolen or damaged.

2. Required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Safety glasses and gloves when handling or performing maintenance (mandatory).
  • Steel-capped boots when lifting or transporting the unit (mandatory).
  • Nitrile gloves when handling the battery.

3. Pre-Operational Checks

a. Machine Inspection

  • Complete pre-start checklist via Task Tracker QR system.
  • Inspect the chassis for damage, debris buildup, and foreign objects lodged underneath.
  • Check the blades for sharpness and secure mounting. Replace dull or damaged blades.
  • Verify the battery charge level on the display or app.
  • Confirm the boundary wire/RTK signal is active and the mower's designated zone is correct.
  • Test the lift and tilt sensors: lift the mower; blades must stop immediately.
  • Check that the emergency stop button functions.

b. Work Area

  • Walk the mowing zone to remove debris, ball markers, broken tees, and foreign objects that could damage blades.
  • Check for temporary course features (marquees, barriers, event signage) that may block the mower's path.
  • Confirm with the pro shop that the mowing zone is suitable for autonomous operation at the scheduled time.

4. Operation

a. Start-Up

  • Place the mower in its starting zone as defined in the scheduling app.
  • Activate the mowing program via the app or the mower's control panel.
  • Confirm the mower begins operating within its defined boundary.

b. During Operation

  • Monitor the mower via the app for status alerts during the mowing session.
  • If the mower gets stuck, approach it cautiously from the front (blade side is underneath, away from you).
  • Press the emergency stop before lifting or moving the mower.
  • Do not attempt to clear debris from under the mower while it is active.

c. Golf Course Specific Rules

  • The mower must not operate in zones where golfers are actively playing unless a risk assessment has been completed and approved by the superintendent.
  • Place warning signs at the entry points to the mowing zone when the robotic mower is operating.
  • The superintendent will define permitted operating times to minimise conflict with play.
  • Report any golfer complaints or near-misses immediately.

5. Emergency Procedures

a. Fire

  • Press emergency stop. If a battery fire starts, do not use water. Use dry chemical extinguisher or sand.
  • Evacuate the area and call 000.
  • Refer to the Lithium-Ion Batteries and Equipment SWP.

b. Injury

  • Press emergency stop. Administer first aid.
  • Call 000 for serious injuries. Site: 825 Cranbourne-Frankston Rd, Cranbourne VIC 3977.
  • Report in Task Tracker.

c. Equipment-Specific Emergency

  • If the mower leaves its boundary, press the emergency stop remotely via the app or physically on the unit.
  • If the mower enters a water hazard or bunker, do not attempt to retrieve it while it is powered on. Press emergency stop first.

6. Post-Operational Procedures

  • Return the mower to its charging station or designated storage location.
  • Clean grass buildup from the underside (blades stopped, power off).
  • Inspect blades for damage.
  • Charge the battery as per schedule.
  • Report faults in Task Tracker.

7. Maintenance and Servicing

  • Daily: clean underside, check blade condition.
  • Weekly: inspect boundary wire/RTK base station, test safety sensors.
  • Monthly: replace blades as per manufacturer schedule.
  • Firmware updates as directed by the superintendent.
  • All servicing recorded in Task Tracker.

8. Relevant Legislation

  • Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic).
  • Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic).
  • AS/NZS 4024.1 Safety of Machinery series.
  • EPA Victoria noise regulations - Environment Protection Act 2017.
  • Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations 2012 (Vic) where applicable.

9. Record Keeping

  • Pre-start checklists completed daily via Task Tracker QR system.
  • All faults, repairs, and servicing recorded in Task Tracker.
  • Incident and near-miss reports submitted through Task Tracker.
  • Training records maintained for all operators with superintendent sign-off.
